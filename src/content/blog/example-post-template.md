Start with a two-sentence answer to the question in the title. AI answer engines and Google's AI Overviews quote short, direct answers.

## Ask the reader's question as a heading

Answer it in the first one or two sentences under the heading, in plain words. Then add detail, steps or examples.

## Use lists and tables for steps and comparisons

1. One step per line.
2. Specific numbers and names, not vague claims.

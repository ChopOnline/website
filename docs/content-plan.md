# Blog content plan (SEO + AEO)

**Status:** v1, 22 Oct 2026. Owner: Rav. Posts are drafted by Claude and merged by Rav or Jas.

## What the blog is for
Bring search and AI-answer traffic to ChopOnline products. Every post answers one real question and links to one product once.

| Pillar | Audience | Sends traffic to | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Gifting | UK gift buyers (Secret Santa, tea lovers, dog mums, cat lovers) | Etsy mugs and hoodies (`pod-shop`) | Now (Christmas) |
| 2. Organised family life | Time-poor working parents (Nina's audience) | Payhip digital products | After the first Payhip product exists |

The author is always ChopOnline, never an AI persona (Nina etc.). A fictional author on advice content misleads readers and weakens trust signals.

## Why gifting first
- UK gift searches are seasonal: a 2021 analysis of UK Google Trends found most Christmas gift terms get over 80% of their yearly volume in October to December ([Scribbler](https://www.scribbler.com/Blog/Christmas-Shopping-Statistics/)).
- New pages take weeks to be indexed and ranked, so Christmas posts need to be live by early November.
- The Etsy products already exist, so each post has something to link to.

## What the evidence says about AI answers (AEO)
- **Rank first.** Ahrefs found 76% of URLs cited in Google AI Overviews rank in Google's top 10 ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/)). Normal SEO still comes first.
- **Answer high up.** AirOps reports 55% of AI citations come from the top 30% of a page ([AirOps](https://www.airops.com/blog/cited-google-ai-overviews-framework)).
- **Short answers under each heading.** About 40–60 words, quotable without context ([Averi](https://www.averi.ai/blog/google-ai-overviews-optimization-how-to-get-featured-in-2026), vendor analysis).
- **Mentions elsewhere.** AI engines weigh brand mentions on Reddit, YouTube and other sites. The AI creators' content helps here.
- ChatGPT's citations rarely match Google's rankings (Ahrefs), so also submit the site to **Bing Webmaster Tools**.

Most of these sources are SEO vendors; treat the figures as direction, not law.
